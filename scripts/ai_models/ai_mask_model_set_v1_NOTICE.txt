Alcedo Studio AI Mask model set, version 1
==========================================

This package contains the AI Mask models of Alcedo Studio. The models are not part of the
Alcedo Studio installer. Alcedo Studio downloads this package from the Alcedo download server
(https://static.aoraw.org) when the user installs the AI Mask models on the AI Models settings
page. package-inventory.json lists the size and SHA-256 of every file of the package.
model_set.json names the model set version and the revision of the weights.

The license texts are in the licenses/ folder:

- licenses/SAM2-Apache-2.0.txt: Apache License 2.0 (SAM 2.1)
- licenses/S3OD-MIT.txt: MIT License (S3OD)
- licenses/DINOv3-License.md: DINOv3 License (the DINOv3 encoder in S3OD)
- licenses/GPL-3.0.txt: GNU General Public License version 3 (the models trained by Alcedo Studio)


1. SAM 2.1 Hiera tiny: image encoder, prompt encoder, and mask decoder
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Files: point_encoder.onnx and point_decoder.onnx (Windows); point_encoder.mlmodelc and
point_decoder.mlmodelc (macOS).

Source: Segment Anything Model 2 (SAM 2) by Meta Platforms, Inc.,
https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam2, checkpoint sam2.1_hiera_tiny.pt (SHA-256
7402e0d864fa82708a20fbd15bc84245c2f26dff0eb43a4b5b93452deb34be69).
License: Apache License 2.0 (licenses/SAM2-Apache-2.0.txt).
Changes: Alcedo Studio converted the checkpoint to ONNX (float32) and to Core ML (float32). The
weights are not changed.
Training data: Meta trained SAM 2 on its SA-1B and SA-V datasets and on other video data (SAM 2
paper, arXiv 2408.00714).


2. S3OD (s3od-synth): Subject
-----------------------------

Files: subject_masks.onnx (Windows); s3od.mlmodelc (macOS).

Source: S3OD by the S3OD Team, https://github.com/KupynOrest/s3od, weights okupyn/s3od-synth
(https://huggingface.co/okupyn/s3od-synth).
License: MIT License, Copyright (c) 2024 S3OD Team (licenses/S3OD-MIT.txt). The image encoder of
S3OD is DINOv3 ViT-B/16 by Meta Platforms, Inc., fine-tuned by the S3OD Team. DINOv3 is licensed
under the DINOv3 License (licenses/DINOv3-License.md, https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov3).
Changes: Alcedo Studio converted the weights to ONNX (float16; the first encoder layer attention in
float32; the attention computed in 8 query chunks) and to Core ML (float16; the patch embedding
and the encoder attention in float32). The weights are not trained again.

Training data lineage (from the S3OD paper, arXiv 2510.21605, section 4.1, and the model card):

- The s3od-synth model is trained only on synthetic images. The S3OD Team generated the images
  with FLUX.1 Krea [dev] (Black Forest Labs), which is released under the FLUX.1 [dev]
  Non-Commercial License. That license has terms for the output of the image generator.
- The masks of the training images come from a teacher decoder that the S3OD Team supervised with
  the DIS5K, HR-SOD, UHRSD, and DUTS datasets. These datasets have their own terms of use.

Alcedo Studio does not state whether these terms apply to the S3OD weights or to their output.
You decide whether the model and its output can be used for your purpose.


3. People and sky locator (people and sky head)
-----------------------------------------------

Files: people_sky_head.onnx (Windows) or people_sky_head.mlmodelc (macOS), and its thresholds
people_sky_head.json.

Source: trained by Alcedo Studio (alcedo-ai-mask-lab, P1 run 2, epoch 15). The head reads the
image_embed output of the SAM 2.1 tiny image encoder. It contains no SAM 2.1 weights.
License: GNU General Public License version 3 (licenses/GPL-3.0.txt), the license of Alcedo Studio.
Training data: COCO 2017 (train split) and Open Images V7 photos with their person and sky labels.
The COCO annotations are licensed under CC BY 4.0, and the use of the COCO images is subject to the
Flickr Terms of Use. The Open Images annotations are licensed under CC BY 4.0, and the images are listed
as having a CC BY 2.0 license.


4. Sky model (sky head)
-----------------------

Files: sky_head.onnx (Windows); sky_head.mlmodelc (macOS).

Source: trained by Alcedo Studio (alcedo-ai-mask-lab, issue 10 run 1, epoch 15). The head reads
the outputs of the SAM 2.1 tiny image encoder and the photo. It contains no SAM 2.1 weights.
License: GNU General Public License version 3 (licenses/GPL-3.0.txt), the license of Alcedo Studio.
Training data: the MaSS13K dataset (CVPR 2025, https://github.com/xiechenxi99/mass13k): 13,348
smartphone photos at 4K with matting-level labels. The dataset repository does not state a clear
license: its README says that the project is released under the Apache 2.0 license, but the
repository has no LICENSE file, and the dataset has no separate license. For commercial use of the
Sky model or its output, contact the authors of the dataset.

Alcedo Studio does not guarantee that the models it trains can be used for commercial work.
