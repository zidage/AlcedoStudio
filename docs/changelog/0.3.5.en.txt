Alcedo Studio 0.3.5

Before you upgrade
------------------
- Advanced Content Analysis is removed. Existing results stay in your projects.
  In Settings > Saved AI Keys, delete saved API keys and disconnect the Codex
  login used by that feature. This cleanup page is available for this release
  only and will be removed in the next version.

New
---
- Film Print: simulate 30 negative films and five color reversal films, with
  searchable stock browsers, brand filters, and color swatches. Combine negatives
  with photographic papers and cinema print stocks, including a separate Cine
  Look (2383), or view reversal films on a light table. Print and slide output
  uses Rec. 709 SDR. The panel remembers your last film settings and groups its
  controls into folding sections with help text. CUDA, OpenCL, and Metal support
  the film workflow, with the film data included in the installer. alcedo-cli
  adds film.list to select films for scripted editing and rendering.
- Negative Scan: scan and invert the simulated negative, then use OpenDRT for
  SDR or HDR output. Adjust scan exposure, contrast, cyan, magenta, yellow, black,
  and the virtual scanner. New scans use Neutral auto color at 50% strength;
  zooming and panning keep the color balance of the full frame. Scene Light
  defaults to As Shot and follows the RAW white balance temperature and tint
  across prints, scans, and reversal films.
- Film format and grain: choose 16mm, 35mm, or 70mm. Grain size follows the frame
  and image size, and Film Print uses the selected stock's granularity data.
  Smaller formats also enlarge light spread and halation in Film Print. Grain
  strength ranges from 0 to 200%, and its scale stays consistent as you zoom.
  Film Print puts grain into the film layers before printing or scanning. It
  enables grain at 100% when its strength is zero; switching to another method
  clears the grain. Other display methods keep grain in Post Processing.
- Collections and navigation: manage nested collections in a compact tree with
  inline create and rename, drag to move, keyboard controls, and photo counts
  that update after photo changes. The photo menu's Add to submenu contains the
  Export Queue and a scrolling collection tree, with filtering for longer lists.
  In the editor, the mouse wheel zooms at the cursor without Ctrl; trackpad
  scrolling still pans the image.

Fixed
-----
- Lens correction and image loading: preserve more detail during distortion and
  chromatic aberration correction with Lanczos-3 resampling and limited ringing
  near bright edges. Improve automatic lens identification for Panasonic RW2
  files and Nikon lenses identified by lens ID. Fix loading of HDR TIFF files
  whose embedded ICC profile contains both LUT tables and a color-description
  tag, including affected Hasselblad X2D II files.
- Large projects and saves: open .alcd projects with database payloads above
  2 GiB. Save and open stream the database instead of holding a whole copy in
  memory. A failed database snapshot no longer blocks later saves. Failed saves
  remove their temporary package files, and startup removes abandoned project
  workspaces and database snapshots while keeping workspaces in use.
