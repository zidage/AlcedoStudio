Alcedo Studio 0.3.6

Before you upgrade
------------------
- Shadows, Highlights, Whites, and Blacks use new tone math, Shadows and
  Highlights now apply before the Tone Curve, and the Tone Curve renders the
  smooth curve that the panel draws. Photos edited in earlier versions with
  these controls can look different when you open them.
- New RAW imports use a new default look (see below). Photos that are already
  in a project keep their current settings.

New
---
- Tone Curve: edit the RGB curve and separate red, green, and blue curves on a
  new plot that shows the histogram of the curve input and stop marks from
  middle grey. Points follow the pointer directly; a right click removes a
  point. Eight presets: Linear, Soft, Medium, and Strong Contrast, Cl. Digit,
  and curves sampled from Kodak 2383, Velvia 50, and Ektachrome E100.
- Default look: new RAW imports use the Cl. Digit curve, a classic digital
  tone shaped after the RawTherapee default curve, for a brighter and less
  flat first view. In Settings > Default Behavior > RAW Import, choose the
  exposure, saturation, Tone Curve preset, display transform, and lens
  correction that new RAW imports receive. Each row has its own reset.
- Camera profiles (DCP): set a DCP folder in Settings > LUTs and Color
  Management. The Camera Profile section of the RAW panel matches the profiles
  in that folder to the camera, and the tone curve of the selected profile
  loads into the Tone Curve for further adjustment. DNG files keep their own
  calibration with a profile. Bring your own DCP files, for example the
  CameraProfiles folder of a Lightroom Classic installation or profiles from
  Adobe DNG Converter. The processing differs from Adobe Camera Raw and the
  camera, so the result can look different from theirs.
- Windows color management: the editor, the comparison view, LUT previews, and
  the Library, filmstrip, and search thumbnails convert to the ICC profile of
  the monitor that shows them. Settings > LUTs and Color Management shows the
  profile in use.
- Sliders: every slider has a new design with the handle inside the rail, a
  value field for typing, and double-click reset. Exposure drags from -5 to
  +5 EV and accepts -10 to +10 EV. Highlights accepts down to -150 and Shadows
  up to +150 for scenes with high contrast. The Selective Color rails show the
  colors of the selected hue.
- Shadows and tone zones: the Shadows lift has a new curve that keeps black,
  so strong lifts no longer turn the shadows grey or brighter than the
  midtones. Whites and Blacks change only their own side of middle grey, by up
  to 3 stops, and negative Whites values now darken highlights.
- Library layout: Justified rows show the photos in rows of one height that
  keep each photo's shape, with camera, exposure, date, labels, and rating on
  hover. Zoom keeps the photos in view in place. Switch between Justified rows
  and Classic grid in Settings > Language / Theme.
- Color labels and the Delete mark: give photos one or more of seven color
  labels and mark photos for deletion. Name the labels in Settings > Library >
  Color Labels, filter by them with By Custom Label, and set labels, the Delete
  mark, and ratings for the whole selection in the Inspector. alcedo-cli reads
  and writes color labels and the Delete mark.
- Library speed: scrolling and zooming large libraries is much faster. In a
  2,943-photo test library, one scroll step in Justified rows takes 4.6 ms
  instead of 72 ms, and cached thumbnails return in 3 ms after a zoom instead
  of 263 ms. AI labels are generated about 16 times faster from the camera
  previews, and the editor frees its GPU memory while labels are generated.
- Library tools: drag files or folders onto the Library or onto a collection
  to import them. Inspector overview sections fold, and Camera, Labels, and
  Lens have a search field. Add to Export Queue opens the Export tab.
- Layers: hide a Color Grade layer with the eye button on its row; hidden
  layers skip processing. A Color Grade without connections stays in the
  document. Show selection in the Mask panel starts on for each Mask.
- Crop: rotate from the zones outside the frame corners, and a finer grid
  appears while you rotate. Drags near the frame edges resize the frame.
- Lens correction: Auto mode now finds the built-in lens of the Fujifilm
  GFX100RF, Ricoh GR III, and Ricoh GR IIIx.
- Logs: Settings > About opens the log folder. The AI process writes its log
  to the same folder, and old log sessions are removed.

Fixed
-----
- Selection: a filter or search change removes the photos it hides from the
  selection, so Delete, ratings, labels, and Paste Adjustments act only on
  photos in the current list. The filmstrip menu acts on every selected photo,
  and a right click on a photo outside the selection acts on that photo alone.
  The editor keeps the open photo when the collection or filter changes.
- Export queue: a photo exported once can be queued again and shows as Queued
  until its next export.
- Filmstrip: arrow keys, Home, and End keep the focused photo in view.
- Library: Ctrl + wheel forward shows fewer, larger photos.
- Tone Curve: every label of the section shows in full.
- alcedo-cli: render.preview writes the root image with its own Develop
  settings, so Lens Correction shows as a change.
